## 4. Honest Scope & Limitations

- **DATA:** **DEMONSTRATION / SIMULATION** — Telemetry records are generated by physical simulation models (`scripts/generate_sample_data.py`), not physical industrial loggers.
- **ENERGY:** **ESTIMATED / SIMULATED** — Energy consumption is derived from empirical thermal-lift equations, not metered by physical electrical hardware.
- **REAL-WORLD VALIDATION:** **PARTIAL** — Actuator recommendations represent advisory decision-support setpoints; no physical refrigeration compressor was actuated.
