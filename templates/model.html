MAD-TCN Architectural Features
Deep Learning
Multi-Scale (M) Captures short-term sensor shocks (door openings) and long-term diurnal cycles simultaneously.
Adaptive (A) Feature-attention weighting across 12 engineered IoT variables (temperature delta, rolling mean, rolling std, ambient diff).
Dilated (D) Exponential dilation factors $d \in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}$ expand receptive field without ballooning parameter count.
Temporal Convolutions (TCN) Strictly causal convolutions prevent lookahead bias; future readings cannot leak into past filters.
Residual Blocks Weight normalization, ReLU non-linearities, and spatial dropout ensure gradient stability.
