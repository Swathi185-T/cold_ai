# Experimental Validation Results

## 1. Dataset & Provenance

- **Provenance:** Simulation cold-chain dataset (`data/raw/simulated_cold_chain.csv`) generated by `scripts/generate_sample_data.py`.
- **Fleet Volume:** 12,000 synthetic timeseries records across 3 warehouse sensor nodes (`CC-01`, `CC-02`, `CC-03`), plus verified user-uploaded telemetry records in SQLite.
- **Input Dimension:** 12 engineered features (`temperature`, `humidity`, `ambient_temperature`, `power_kw`, `compressor_state`, `door_open`, `temperature_delta`, `rolling_mean`, `rolling_std`, `temperature_slope`, `ambient_difference`, `power_delta`).
- **Temporal Sequences:** Input shape $60 \times 12$ (60 past timesteps) mapped to 15 future steps.

---

## 2. Model Performance (MAD-TCN)

Evaluated against held-out simulation test sequences:

- **Architecture:** Multi-scale Adaptive Dilated Temporal Convolutional Network
- **Input Shape:** $60 \times 12$
- **Forecast Horizon:** 15 steps
- **MAE (Mean Absolute Error):** `1.2500958442687988`
- **RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error):** `1.467206597328186`
- **Device:** CPU (PyTorch)
- **Checkpoint:** `models/mad_tcn_best.pt` (~89 KB, loaded with weights_only=True)

---

## 3. Risk Engine Results

For sensor `CC-01` with observed temperature $T_0 = 3.9327\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ against standard Food profile limits ($0.0 - 5.0\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$):
- **15-Step Forecast Trajectory:** Ranging from $3.676\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ to $5.136\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$ (mean $4.379\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$).
- **Future Risk Score:** `0.42` / 100
- **Risk Level:** `LOW`
- **Breach Horizon:** Detected potential upward excursion across profile threshold at step $t+2$ ($5.14\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$).
- **Risk Explanation:** Evaluates forecast boundary excursion and temperature velocity rather than static instantaneous temperature.

## 5. Comparative Evaluation: Current-State vs. Prediction-Guided

| Evaluation Dimension | Reactive Current-State Optimization | Prediction-Guided Optimization (COLDCHAIN AI) | Factual Difference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Observation Metric** | Current temperature only ($T_0 = 3.93\text{ }^\circ\text{C}$) | 15-step forecast $\hat{T}_{t+1..t+15}$ via MAD-TCN | Temporal lookahead |
| **Detected Risk Score** | **0.00** (inside 0–5°C bound; no violation detected) | **0.42** (detected future excursion to 5.14°C at $t+2$) | Early detection before threshold cross |

## 6. Known Research Limitations

1. **Simulation Data:** The dataset is generated through thermal modeling rather than physical cold-room loggers.
2. **Simulated Energy Model:** Energy values are computed via an empirical thermal-lift formula rather than physical kWh metering.
3. **Execution Environment:** PyTorch inference executes on CPU; GPU acceleration is not evaluated.
4. **Actuator Validation:** Control outputs are decision-support setpoints; no physical PLC or refrigeration compressor was modulated during validation.
